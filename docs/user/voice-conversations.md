# Voice conversations

Use the waveform button beside the composer to enable hands-free voice conversations with Mai.
Say “Hey Mai” once to wake the conversation, then speak commands naturally. Pause briefly to send
each command.

The waveform stays listening while Mai works and speaks. To change direction, start speaking again:
Mai’s current audio and provider turn stop immediately, then your new command is captured and sent
after you pause.

Press Escape or say “go to sleep” to return to wake-phrase-only listening. Click the waveform again
to turn voice capture off. Browser microphone permission and the configured local voice service are
required.
